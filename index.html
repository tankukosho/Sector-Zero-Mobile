How to play

DragDrag anywhere to look around. You can also slide your thumb while holding L or R. Hold LLeft hand grabs the hold in the centre of the screen Hold RRight hand grabs. Let go of the button to release. LungeThrow yourself off the wall where you're looking. Hold L or R to catch the next hold. PitonHammer a piton into the wall in front of you to make your own hold WalkWalk forward on ledges. Grab the piton packs on them.

Climb hand over hand: grab the next hold, then let go of the old one. Rusted rungs give way shortly after you grab them. Ledges let you rest and have piton packs on them.

Watch out for things living in the shaft. Denizens crawl toward your hands and bite them off the wall, but they hate light: look straight at one and it flees. Leeches sit on holds, so don't grab those. In the Pump Works, steam vents hiss and then burst, scalding your grip.
